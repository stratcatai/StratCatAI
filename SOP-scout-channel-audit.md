# SOP — Scout: StratCatAI Channel & Positioning Audit

**Scout** = Claude running in the Chrome browser extension, with live access to the
YouTube channel and YouTube Studio. This SOP tells Scout exactly what to check,
what to fix on his own, and what to leave for Strat to decide.

**Run it by:** opening YouTube Studio in Chrome, then telling Scout: *"Run the
Channel Audit SOP."* Scout works top to bottom and ends with two lists (see Output).

---

## 0. Prime directive

Everything on the channel must match the **StratCatAI** brand. When something is
off:
- **Fix it** if it's a clear-cut consistency error — spelling, brand-name casing,
  a wrong or dead link, a retired term.
- **Flag it** (don't touch) if it's a judgment call — a title that's performing,
  a thumbnail choice, tone, or anything where changing it is a creative decision.

**Never** invent income numbers or claims. **Never** soften the skeptical,
anti-hype voice into guru-speak — that's the exact thing the channel debunks.

---

## 1. Autonomy + the Change Log (do this every time)

Scout **may make edits directly** in YouTube Studio for clear-cut fixes.

But for **every single edit**, Scout keeps a running **Change Log** entry:

> `[date] — [where: video title / channel section] — "before" → "after" — why`

Nothing gets changed silently. At the end, Scout reports the full Change Log so
Strat can see exactly what moved. Anything that's a creative or strategy call goes
in the separate **"Needs Strat's Call"** list instead of being edited.

---

## 2. Brand canon (what "correct" means)

- **Name:** StratCatAI — one word. Personal byline/nickname: **StratCat**.
- **Handle:** `@stratcatai` everywhere.
- **Verdict system:** **HIRED** *(works)* / **WRITTEN UP** *(works, but…)* /
  **FIRED** *(doesn't work)*. Never a verdict without real numbers behind it.
- **Voice:** skeptical, blunt, results-first, dry humor. Receipts over promises.
  Kill hype language ("passive income on autopilot," "this will change your life,"
  fake urgency, "one weird trick").
- **The operator arc:** the "19 years running restaurants → built a BS detector →
  now pointed at AI" story is the channel's **throughline**. Scout checks that it
  shows up in the **channel-level positioning** (About, trailer, banner tagline).
  Scout does **NOT** try to force "19 years in restaurants" into every individual
  video — flag it only if it's *missing from the overall channel story*, not if a
  single video doesn't mention it.

---

## 3. Spelling & casing traps — auto-fix these on sight

- **GitHub** — capital G and H. Fix "Github", "github", "Get Hub", "get hub".
- **StratCatAI** — one word, that casing. Fix "Strat Cat AI", "StratCat AI",
  "Stratcat AI", "stratcatai" (in prose).
- **Claude Code** — capital C, capital C.
- **SkeptikalKat** — RETIRED. If found anywhere, replace with **StratCat**.
- Old positioning: **"AI for restaurant operators"** / "AI tools for operators" is
  the retired angle. Flag it if it appears in channel copy (banner/About) — the
  operator *backstory* stays, but the channel is no longer "AI for operators."

---

correctly, and actually load (no 404). A wrong/dead link is a clear-cut fix.

---

## 5. The audit checklist

### A. Channel shell (do this first)
- [ ] Channel name is exactly **StratCatAI**; handle is **@stratcatai**
- [ ] Banner reflects current positioning (NOT "AI for restaurant operators")
- [ ] Avatar / logo present and on-brand
- [ ] **About** section: matches the approved channel description, operator arc
      present, no hype, links correct
- [ ] Channel **Links** section: all canonical links, all resolve
- [ ] A featured/trailer video is set

### B. Every video — shorts AND long-form (go one by one)
For each video:
- [ ] **Title:** spelling clean, brand casing correct, no hype/guru language,
      promises only what the video delivers (honest-result rule)
- [ ] **Description:** first line carries the video (YouTube hides the rest behind
      "…more"); links present, correct, and resolving; timestamps on long-form;
      soft CTA at the end
- [ ] **Consistency:** verdict language correct if used (HIRED/WRITTEN UP/FIRED);
      voice on-brand
- [ ] **Shorts only:** `#Shorts` present in the description (that's what flags it as
      a Short on YouTube), vertical, hook readable in the first 2 seconds
- [ ] **Thumbnail:** FLAG ONLY (judgment call) if it looks off-brand — don't change

---

## 6. Output (how Scout reports back)

End every audit with exactly two lists:

**1. Changes Made** — the full Change Log (before → after, where, why).

**2. Needs Strat's Call** — everything Scout did NOT touch because it's a judgment
or strategy decision (title rewrites on performing videos, thumbnail swaps, tone
calls, missing operator arc at the channel level, etc.), each with a one-line
recommendation.

Keep both lists tight and skimmable. Strat should be able to read the whole thing
in one pass and either nod or make the few calls that are actually his.
